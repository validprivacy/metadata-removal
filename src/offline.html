Metadata Removal

Remove location, device details, dates, and other hidden data from photos, videos, and audio. This file runs entirely on your own computer, with no internet connection needed, and your files are never uploaded.

Drop files or folders here

JPEG, HEIC, PNG, WebP, MP4, MOV, M4A, MP3, WAV, and FLAC. Your originals are never changed.

Lossless. Only the metadata is removed. The picture, video, and audio stay byte for byte the same, and phone photos stay upright.

It cannot remove what is in the picture or recording itself, such as faces, signs, or voices. Some drones save GPS in a separate .SRT file next to the video, so leave that file out when you share.

Coming soon: AVIF images, GIF, TIFF, RAW camera files, PDFs, and Word, Excel, and PowerPoint documents.
